# OIBSIP - Data Analytics Internship

## Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

### 📌 Project Overview

This project is completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset using Python. The analysis focuses on identifying sales patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, and relationships between numerical variables.

### 🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Understand the structure and characteristics of the retail sales dataset.
* Perform data inspection and cleaning.
* Calculate descriptive statistics.
* Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
* Study customer demographics such as age and gender.
* Identify top-selling products.
* Analyze revenue by product category.
* Study correlations between numerical variables.
* Identify additional business insights through data visualization.
* Provide actionable business recommendations based on the analysis.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

### 📊 Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed in this project:

1. **Initial Dataset Inspection**

   * Dataset shape
   * Column data types
   * Missing/null values
   * Basic dataset information

2. **Descriptive Statistics**

   * Mean
   * Median
   * Mode
   * Standard deviation

3. **Time Series Analysis**

   * Monthly sales trends
   * Quarterly sales trends

4. **Customer Demographics**

   * Customer age group distribution
   * Gender distribution

5. **Product Analysis**

   * Top 10 best-selling products
   * Revenue by product category

6. **Correlation Analysis**

   * Correlation matrix
   * Numerical variable relationship analysis using a heatmap

7. **Additional Visualization**

   * An additional visualization was created to identify a non-obvious pattern or business insight from the dataset.

### 📈 Key Visualizations

The project includes:

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Quarterly Sales Trend
* Customer Age Group Distribution
* Gender Distribution
* Top 10 Best-Selling Products
* Revenue by Product Category
* Correlation Heatmap
* Additional Business Insight Visualization

### 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Changes in sales over time.
* Customer demographic patterns.
* Product categories contributing to revenue.
* Best-selling products.
* Relationships between sales-related numerical variables.
* Potential areas for improving sales and business performance.

### 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings from the EDA, specific recommendations are provided to:

* Focus on high-performing products and categories.
* Plan inventory based on sales trends.
* Use customer demographic insights for targeted marketing.
* Identify low-performing products for further analysis.
* Use historical sales patterns for better business planning.

> **Note:** The final recommendations are based on the actual findings obtained from the dataset and visualizations in the Jupyter Notebook.
