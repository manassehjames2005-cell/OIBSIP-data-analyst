# Task 1 – Retail Sales EDA

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

### 📌 Objective

The objective of this project is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, and meaningful business insights.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **1,000 retail transactions** and **9 columns**.

### Dataset Features

* Transaction ID
* Date
* Customer ID
* Gender
* Age
* Product Category
* Quantity
* Price per Unit
* Total Amount

The dataset was inspected and prepared for analysis using Python and Pandas.

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Dataset Inspection

Performed an initial inspection of the dataset:

* Checked dataset shape
* Checked column names
* Checked data types
* Checked for missing values
* Converted the `Date` column to datetime format

**Dataset Shape:** `1000 rows × 9 columns`

No missing values were found in the dataset.

---

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Calculated descriptive statistics for numerical columns, including:

* Mean
* Median
* Mode
* Standard Deviation

The analysis helped understand the distribution and variation of sales-related numerical variables.

---

### 3. Time Series Analysis

Analyzed sales performance over time using:

* Monthly sales trends
* Quarterly sales trends
* Line charts

This analysis helps identify changes in sales performance across different time periods.

---

### 4. Customer Demographics Analysis

Analyzed customer demographics based on:

* Customer age groups
* Gender distribution

Visualizations were created to understand the characteristics of the customer base.

---

### 5. Product Analysis

Performed product-level analysis including:

* Top 10 best-selling products
* Revenue by product category
* Product category comparison using bar charts

This analysis helps identify products and categories contributing significantly to sales.

---

### 6. Correlation Analysis

Created a **correlation heatmap** to understand relationships between numerical variables such as:

* Age
* Quantity
* Price per Unit
* Total Amount

The heatmap helps identify the strength and direction of relationships between numerical variables.

---

### 7. Additional Visualization

Created an additional visualization to identify a non-obvious pattern in the retail sales data.

The visualization was used to understand customer purchasing behaviour and identify useful business insights beyond the basic analysis.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

The EDA helped identify important patterns related to:

* Sales performance over time
* Customer age distribution
* Gender-wise customer distribution
* Product category performance
* Top-selling products
* Relationships between numerical variables
* Customer purchasing behaviour

Observations were added after each visualization in the Jupyter Notebook.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Focus on high-performing product categories** to maintain strong revenue contribution.

2. **Use customer demographic insights** to design targeted marketing campaigns for different customer groups.

3. **Monitor monthly and quarterly sales trends** to identify periods of higher or lower sales and plan promotions accordingly.
